Water Glossary

TDS — Total Dissolved Solids
Milligrams of dissolved minerals per litre. Lower TDS (under 100 mg/L) tastes lighter; higher TDS often brings fuller, mineral-forward character.
pH Level
Measures acidity vs. alkalinity on a 0–14 scale. 7 is neutral. Sparkling waters are more acidic (5–6); many still waters are slightly alkaline (7–8).
Artesian Aquifer
A confined underground layer where water is naturally pressurised and rises without pumping — protected from surface contamination.
Spring Water
Flows naturally to the surface from a subsurface aquifer. Must emerge at the same point of origin and be collected at the source or via a borehole.
Naturally Sparkling
Contains CO₂ that occurs naturally in the source, sometimes supplemented with carbonation from the same source during bottling.
Silica
A mineral often credited with benefits to skin, hair, and nails. Found in notably high concentrations in Volvic water from volcanic basalt.
